# Project Supplementary Student Regulations
## Student Conduct and Administrative Procedures

> **Corpus status:** Synthetic project material created for controlled RuleGuard testing. It is not an official Medi-Caps University policy.

### 1. General Conduct

Students are expected to follow reasonable instructions issued through authorised university channels and to conduct themselves respectfully in academic and residential environments.

### 2. Identity

Students may be required to carry an institutional identity card for specified academic, examination, laboratory, hostel, or placement activities. The exact requirement should be taken from the rule applicable to that activity.

### 3. Misconduct

Misconduct may include behaviour expressly prohibited by an applicable policy. The consequences depend on the relevant rule and disciplinary procedure.

### 4. Complaints

A student who wishes to report an administrative or academic issue should use the designated grievance or departmental process. A complaint should state the relevant facts and attach available evidence.

### 5. Evidence

Documents, official acknowledgements, portal records, and other authorised records may be considered in administrative review. Informal statements from other students should not automatically be treated as official decisions.

### 6. Committee Decisions

Where a rule assigns authority to a committee, the committee's decision should be recorded through the applicable procedure. A student should not assume that an individual staff member can exercise a power assigned specifically to a committee.

### 7. Exceptional Consideration

A student may submit a request for exceptional consideration when the ordinary regulations do not adequately address a documented circumstance. Submission does not guarantee approval.

### 8. Communication

Official decisions should be communicated through an authorised channel. A message saying "it should be okay" is not equivalent to a formal approval when the applicable regulation requires documented approval.

### 9. Data Accuracy

Students should provide accurate information in academic and administrative submissions. Corrections should be made through the prescribed procedure rather than by altering an already-issued document.

### 10. Appeals

Where an appeal mechanism exists, the student should submit the appeal within the applicable period and identify the decision being challenged.

### 11. Privacy

Students should avoid sharing another person's academic record or personal information unnecessarily. This corpus does not create a general entitlement to access another student's confidential record.

### 12. Silence of the Corpus

The absence of a provision should be treated as meaningful for the purposes of this project. If the corpus does not state what happens in a particular situation, the system should classify the question as NOT_COVERED rather than filling the gap from general university practice.

### 13. RuleGuard Principle

The system's job is evidence retrieval and grounded response generation, not policy invention. The answer should be supported by retrieved passages and should show the source and section for each material claim.
