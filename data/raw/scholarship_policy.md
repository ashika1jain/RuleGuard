# Project Supplementary Student Regulations
## Scholarship and Financial Assistance Policy

> **Corpus status:** Synthetic project material created for controlled RuleGuard testing. It is not an official Medi-Caps University policy.

### 1. Purpose

This policy defines a scholarship framework covering eligibility, renewal, documentation, review, suspension, and administrative handling. It describes common requirements for scholarship applications and renewal.

### 2. Eligibility

A scholarship applicant must satisfy the eligibility conditions stated for the relevant scholarship category. Conditions may include academic performance, enrolment status, financial documentation, attendance, or another requirement expressly stated in the scholarship notice.

A student should not assume that satisfying one condition guarantees the scholarship. Eligibility is determined using all applicable conditions.

### 3. Application

Scholarship applications should be submitted through the designated process before the stated deadline. An incomplete application may be returned for correction if the procedure permits correction.

The student is responsible for ensuring that uploaded documents are legible and correspond to the requested information. A successful upload does not necessarily mean that the application has been approved.

### 4. Supporting Documents

Supporting documents may include academic records, identity documents, income-related documents, bank information, or other documents identified in the scholarship notice.

The policy does not establish a universal list of documents for every scholarship. Where a required document is not specified for the relevant scholarship, the applicable scholarship notice should be consulted.

### 5. Renewal

A renewal applicant must satisfy the renewal conditions applicable to the scholarship. Renewal is not automatic merely because the student received a scholarship in the previous academic period.

The fee schedule lists a standard scholarship-renewal deadline of 15 August and a late-action date of 22 August. A separate scholarship memorandum states that renewal applications submitted by 20 August will be accepted without late classification. The applicable notice or memorandum should be considered when determining the relevant deadline.

### 6. Academic Performance

Where a scholarship notice specifies an academic-performance threshold, the student's academic record should be evaluated using the method stated in that notice. This corpus does not establish one universal percentage or CGPA threshold for every scholarship.

A student should not be told that a particular CGPA guarantees renewal unless the relevant rule explicitly says so.

### 7. Attendance

Some scholarships may contain an attendance condition, but this document does not create a universal scholarship attendance threshold. Questions about attendance eligibility should be answered from the applicable attendance or scholarship provision.

### 8. Financial Circumstances

Where financial need is part of a scholarship category, the applicant may be required to provide evidence specified by the relevant notice. A student's statement that financial circumstances have changed is not, by itself, a defined numerical eligibility criterion in this corpus.

### 9. Verification

The scholarship authority may verify submitted information. If a discrepancy is identified, the applicant may be asked to provide clarification or additional documentation.

The submission of a document for verification does not automatically establish that the document has been accepted as valid.

### 10. Multiple Scholarships

This corpus does not establish a universal rule allowing or prohibiting every combination of scholarships. A question about combining two named scholarships should be answered only if a provision addresses that combination.

### 11. Disbursement

Approved scholarship amounts may be disbursed according to the administrative schedule applicable to the scholarship. Approval and disbursement are separate events.

A student should not interpret an application acknowledgement as proof that payment has been released.

### 12. Bank Information

Where bank information is required, the student should provide the information in the format prescribed by the scholarship process. Errors may delay processing.

The corpus does not specify a particular bank, branch, or payment application as universally mandatory.

### 13. Suspension

A scholarship may be suspended where the applicable scholarship rules provide for suspension. A suspension decision should identify the reason and applicable procedure.

The corpus does not state that every disciplinary action automatically cancels every scholarship.

### 14. Withdrawal from a Programme

A student who withdraws from a programme may have scholarship consequences under the applicable scholarship conditions. This document does not establish a universal refund or repayment formula.

### 15. Change of Programme

A change of programme may affect scholarship eligibility where the scholarship category is tied to a specific programme. Students should seek a determination through the designated process.

### 16. Change of Financial Circumstances

Where a scholarship is awarded on the basis of financial need, a material change may need to be reported if the applicable notice requires it. The corpus does not define a universal monetary threshold for what counts as material.

### 17. Deadline Interpretation

A standard deadline and a late-action deadline are different concepts. The existence of a late-action date does not necessarily mean that the application will receive the same treatment as an on-time application.

The separate 15 August and 20 August provisions provide different dates and conditions for scholarship renewal. Both should be considered when determining the applicable deadline.

### 18. Late Submission

A late application may require additional approval, documentation, or review when the relevant notice says so. The corpus does not state that every late application must be rejected.

### 19. Appeal

Where a scholarship decision is subject to an appeal process, the applicant should follow the deadline and submission method stated in the relevant notice. This corpus does not provide a universal appeal period.

### 20. Correction of an Application

If an applicant discovers an error before the correction period closes, the applicant should use the prescribed correction mechanism. Sending a second application does not automatically cancel the first application unless the procedure says so.

### 21. Duplicate Applications

This policy does not establish a universal penalty for submitting duplicate applications. Any consequence depends on the applicable procedure.

### 22. False Information

Submitting false or fabricated information may lead to review under the applicable academic or administrative procedure. The exact consequence depends on the relevant rule and circumstances.

### 23. Student Responsibility

Students should monitor official notices, maintain copies of submissions, and verify application status. Failure to read a published notice does not automatically extend a deadline.

### 24. Administrative Assistance

A student may ask the designated office for clarification about an application. Informal advice should not be treated as a formal approval where the policy requires a documented decision.

### 25. Committee Review

Where a scholarship category requires committee review, the committee may consider the evidence and applicable criteria. A request for committee review does not guarantee a positive outcome.

### 26. Confidentiality

Scholarship applications may contain sensitive financial and academic information. The corpus does not create a general right for one student to inspect another student's application.

### 27. Records

The scholarship authority may retain records required for administration and verification. Students should retain their own acknowledgement or receipt where available.

### 28. Questions Outside the Corpus

If a scholarship category is not described in this policy, its eligibility and administrative requirements cannot be determined from this document alone. Rules applicable to another scholarship category should not automatically be applied.

### 29. Examples

For scholarship renewal, the fee schedule lists 15 August as a standard deadline. A separate memorandum provides another date for acceptance of renewal applications without late classification.

For a scholarship category not described in this policy, the applicable eligibility and renewal requirements should be obtained from the relevant scholarship notice.

### 30. Policy Interpretation

The scholarship provisions cover several related administrative concepts, including renewal, approval, deadlines, eligibility, and documentation.

Where multiple provisions concern the same administrative action, they should be considered together. Where the available information is incomplete, the applicable scholarship notice or administrative record should be consulted.

### 31. Administrative Status

An application can have statuses such as submitted, under review, approved, returned for correction, rejected, or otherwise recorded according to the process. The exact status should be taken from the record rather than inferred from the student's expectation. A student who has submitted documents should not be told that the scholarship is approved unless the corpus contains an approval provision or status confirming it.

### 32. Deadline Questions

A standard deadline and a late-action date are different concepts. Where multiple dates appear in applicable administrative documents, the dates and their stated conditions should be considered together.

### 33. Corpus Boundary

This synthetic policy does not establish rules for employment contracts, taxation, immigration, transportation, food services, personal banking, or other topics outside university scholarship administration. Questions concerning those subjects should be addressed under the applicable university regulation or administrative procedure.

### 30. RuleGuard Design Principle

The scholarship policy is intentionally broad enough to create realistic retrieval noise. Terms such as renewal, approval, deadline, eligibility, and documentation appear in several sections. The retrieval system must use semantic relevance and metadata rather than relying only on exact keyword matching.

The final generated answer must be grounded in retrieved passages. When the evidence is incomplete, the system should say so. When two applicable provisions disagree, the system should expose the disagreement rather than silently choosing the passage with the highest similarity score.

### 32. Deadline Questions

When a user asks "when is it due?", the system should distinguish the normal deadline from a late-action date whenever both appear. If the question does not specify whether the user means the standard deadline or the last possible submission date, the answer should explain both dates instead of collapsing them into one.

### 33. Corpus Boundary

This synthetic policy does not establish rules for employment contracts, taxation, immigration, transportation, food services, personal banking, or other topics outside university scholarship administration. A plausible question about one of those subjects is not evidence that a rule exists. The correct response is NOT_COVERED unless another corpus document supplies the answer.

### 34. Evaluation Ground Truth

For testing purposes, the scholarship deadline conflict is intentionally preserved even though real administrative policies would normally be expected to reconcile or replace inconsistent notices. RuleGuard must report the evidence it retrieves and must not silently repair the conflict.

### 35. Source Attribution

Every retrieved passage should retain its source filename and section. If a table row is retrieved, the interface should identify the table and relevant row rather than displaying an unexplained number. This allows a reviewer to trace the answer back to the original corpus material.
