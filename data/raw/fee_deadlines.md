> **Corpus status:** Synthetic project material created for controlled RuleGuard testing. It is not an official Medi-Caps University fee notice.

### 1. Fee Communication

Fee amounts and deadlines in this synthetic schedule are included to provide structured tabular data for retrieval. They are not intended to represent current university charges.

### 2. Payment Verification

A student should verify that a payment has been successfully recorded rather than relying only on a bank transaction message. If the portal does not reflect the payment after the stated processing period, the student should use the designated support process.

### 3. Standard Deadline

The standard deadline is the normal date by which the relevant request or payment should be completed. Students should not treat the late-action date as an automatic extension of eligibility where the table's notes require approval.

### 4. Late-Action Date

A late-action date identifies the final date in this synthetic schedule for submitting the item through the late process. A late-action submission may have additional conditions.

### 5. Tuition Confirmation

Semester tuition confirmation is listed as 10 August. A student should check the payment status after completing the transaction.

### 6. Examination Registration

Examination registration is listed as 20 September, with a late-action date of 25 September. This schedule does not establish that every late registration will be accepted automatically.

### 7. Scholarship Renewal

Scholarship renewal is listed as 15 August. Supporting documents should be submitted with the application where required.

### 8. Hostel Renewal

Hostel renewal requests are listed as due on 05 July, with a late-action date of 12 July. The late-action date does not guarantee a room because room availability remains a separate condition.

### 9. Library Clearance

Library clearance is listed as 10 May. Students should resolve outstanding library obligations before requesting services for which clearance is required.

### 10. Project Registration

Project registration is listed as 30 January. A department may require supervisor or coordinator approval.

### 11. Record Requests

Transcript requests and similar academic-record services may have processing times. The date of submission should not automatically be treated as the date of completion.

### 12. Fee Refunds

This corpus does not establish a general refund entitlement for every payment. A refund question should be answered only when the corpus contains a specific applicable provision.

### 13. Deadline Conflict for Evaluation

**Section FD-13.1:** For a controlled conflict case, an administrative notice in the corpus states that scholarship renewal applications must be received by **15 August**.

**Section FD-13.2:** A separate project scholarship memorandum states that scholarship renewal applications submitted by **20 August** will be accepted without late classification.

These two provisions intentionally establish different deadlines for the same renewal action. A question asking for the final non-late scholarship renewal date should therefore be classified as CONFLICT.

### 14. Why the Table Matters

The table gives the retrieval system structured information that can be queried naturally. For example, a user may ask, "When is hostel renewal due?" The system should retrieve the row containing 05 July and 12 July and explain the distinction between standard and late-action dates.

### 15. No Invented Fees

If a user asks for a fee amount that is not present in this schedule or the authentic placement PDF, the system must not guess. The appropriate classification is NOT_COVERED.
